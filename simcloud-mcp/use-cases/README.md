## Before you start

1. [Connect your client](../docs/connect-clients.md).
2. Paste the [policy from Safety and guardrails](../docs/safety-and-guardrails.md#a-policy-to-paste-into-your-assistant) into the assistant's instructions.
3. For any use case that spends, run a R2 test to your own number first.

## How each page is laid out

- **Scenario:** who and why
- **Tools:** what the assistant calls
- **Try it:** prompts to copy
- **What good looks like:** the shape of a correct answer
- **Guardrails:** what to enforce
- **Variations:** where to take it next

The prompts are examples. Names, numbers and amounts are made up.
