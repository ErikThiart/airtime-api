Built for money that cannot be taken back

Airtime, vouchers and electricity tokens are cash. Every guide is written around that.

Never retry a purchase blindly

A timeout means the outcome is unknown. Check the order first. Every sample exits with a distinct code for that case.

Test with no money

Local mock servers follow the WSDL and the published JSON. Samples default to them, so nothing spends money by accident.

Controls for banks

Security and operations guides, a go-live checklist, reconciliation, and no dependencies to vet in the core samples.
